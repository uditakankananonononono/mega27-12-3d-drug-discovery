## RMSD criterion and decision rule (frozen, unchanged from the 12A corrected reanalysis)
Pose semantics as locked in 12A: reactive docking places the UNREACTED ligand
in near-attack geometry; the heavy-atom skeleton does not rearrange on bond
formation, so RMSD is computed against the crystallographic ADDUCT ligand
heavy atoms; threshold 2.0 A. Best-energy pose per seed (lowest estimated
free energy across that seed's nrun models), heavy atoms, symmetry-corrected
minimum over MCS automorphisms computed with the coordinate-identity remap,
no superposition (receptor frame retained). Per complex, PASS iff
best-energy-pose RMSD <= 2.0 A in >= 2 of 3 completed seeds. If fewer than 2
of 3 seeds complete for a complex, that complex is MACHINERY-INCOMPLETE and
reported to parent for adjudication; the decision rule is never evaluated on
partial seeds.
